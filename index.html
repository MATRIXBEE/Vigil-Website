    FAQ

    Good to know.

    Still unsure? Email ike@wearevigil.global

    What is VIGIL Summit 2027?

    The global summit for evidence-led safety leaders: a five-day, live online summit for health, safety and environment professionals, 1–5 March 2027. The days move from Vision to Diagnose, Challenge and Evidence in Practice, ending with Blueprint Day, where VIP attendees turn the week’s evidence into a practical plan.

    Is the event online?

    Yes. VIGIL 2027 is an online summit streamed via Zoom and Heartbeat.

    How do I attend?

    Heartbeat is your home for VIGIL 2027.

    While VIGIL will be streamed across our online channels, registered attendees should join us on Heartbeat for the full summit experience.

    It’s where you’ll find the daily programme, session links, speaker and partner information, VIGIL workbooks and resources, session recordings, and important updates throughout the week.

    Register once. Join Heartbeat. Everything you need for VIGIL will be waiting for you there.

    Which days are free?

    Days 1–4 are free to attend live, with 48-hour access to session recordings. Day 5, Blueprint Day, is for VIP pass holders.

    What is included in VIP?

    VIP includes everything in the Free pass, plus access to the full Blueprint Day experience, including the Roadmap Labs and Masterclasses.

    VIP attendees also receive a CPD certificate and access to the live Zoom rooms, along with the additional summit resources and recording access included in their ticket.

    Can my company send a team?

    Absolutely. VIGIL is designed to be valuable across teams, with different sessions offering perspectives for leaders, managers and practitioners.

    Each registered team member gets their own access to the VIGIL Heartbeat space, including the programme, workbooks and resources, speaker and partner information, event updates, and session recordings included with their ticket.

    Bringing a team also means you can divide and explore different sessions and tracks, then bring the learning back together within your organisation.

    What is VIGIL Checkpoint?

    The VIGIL Checkpoint is our industry diagnostic exploring how HSE professionals and organisations are progressing across Data & Evidence, Digital Tools & Systems, and AI & Automation. It helps us understand what is happening in practice, where progress is being made, where challenges remain and what organisations and professionals are experiencing.

    The findings inform the State of Digital HSE Report and help ground VIGIL conversations in evidence rather than assumptions.

    Take the free 10-minute, 30-question digital HSE maturity assessment, and you will get an archetype, a report sent to your inbox and some recommended next steps. Take the Checkpoint

    Can I speak at VIGIL?

    Our speakers may come from HSE, technology, data, AI, digital transformation, academia and other fields that can contribute meaningfully to the conversations we are exploring.

    Speaker opportunities are curated around the VIGIL programme, so we look for people whose experience and perspective align with the themes and outcomes of each session. Vendors who want to showcase a product should look at partnership instead.

    How can I partner with VIGIL?

    We work with organisations that share VIGIL’s interest in advancing how HSE uses data, technology and digital capability.

    Partnership can take different forms: from strategic and industry partnerships to supporting the event through sponsorship, expertise, resources or audience reach.

    If your organisation would like to explore working with VIGIL, please get in touch with our team. We’ll discuss your objectives and where there may be a meaningful fit. Partner with VIGIL
