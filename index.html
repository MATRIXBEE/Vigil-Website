    FAQ

    Good to know.

    Still unsure? Email ike@wearevigil.global

    What is VIGIL Summit 2027?

    The global summit for evidence-led safety leaders: a five-day, live online summit for health, safety and environment professionals, 1–5 March 2027. Each day follows the five pillars of the VIGIL Checkpoint, ending with a 90-day plan.

    Is the event online?

    Yes. It runs live online, streamed on Zoom, three hours a day for five days: 2:00–5:00pm GMT, which is 9:00am–12:00pm ET and 6:00–9:00pm Gulf (GST). This page shows the times in your own time zone too.

    Which days are free?

    Days 1–4 are free to attend live. Day 5, Roadmap, and the recordings are for VIP pass holders.

    What is included with VIP?

    The VIP pass ($197) adds Day 5, Roadmap, where you build a 90-day plan or join the HSE Digital Skills Masterclass, plus the session recordings.

    How do I attend?

    Register free, then join live on Zoom each day.

    Can my company send a team?

    Yes. Corporate passes start from $3,997 and cover up to five people. Enquire for your team

    What is VIGIL Checkpoint?

    A free, 10-minute, 30-question digital HSE maturity assessment across five pillars. You get an archetype, a pillar radar and next steps. Take the Checkpoint

    Can I speak at VIGIL?

    If you're an HSE leader with evidence to share, yes. Speakers get a free VIP pass, their recording, a promo kit and a profile page. Vendors who want to showcase a product should look at partnership instead. Speak at VIGIL
